# 📖 Minecraft Book & Quill Diary

An interactive, web-based digital diary designed to look and feel exactly like the "Book and Quill" item from Minecraft. Write your daily thoughts, save your survival stories, and flip through your past memories in an authentic 8-bit interface.

## ✨ Features
* **Authentic UI:** Exact replica of the in-game Book & Quill 2-page GUI.
* **Floating Item Cover:** Starts as a floating, animated 3D item that pops open into a readable book.
* **Local Storage:** All entries are saved locally in your browser's memory. No servers, complete privacy.
* **Mobile Responsive:** Adapts to phone screens by stacking the pages vertically for easy typing and reading.
* **Immersive Design:** Uses the `VT323` pixel font, custom Minecraft-style buttons, and a generated dirt-block background.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Click the floating Book & Quill item to open the diary.
2. Use the **Left Page** to write a title and your entry.
3. Click **Sign & Save**.
4. Read your saved memories on the **Right Page** (or bottom page on mobile).
5. Use the **◀ ▶ arrows** to flip through older pages, or click **Delete** to burn a memory.

## 🛠️ Built With
* **HTML5** for structure.
* **CSS3** for the pixel-art UI, animations, and responsive mobile design.
* **Vanilla JavaScript** for logic, page-flipping, and `localStorage` data management.
